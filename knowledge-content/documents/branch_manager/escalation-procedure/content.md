## What Counts as an Escalation

Anything the Q&A assistant cannot resolve for a parent, student, or tutor - and
anything involving safety, wellbeing, or a disputed policy decision - counts as an
escalation.

## Handling an Escalation

When the assistant offers to escalate, it prepares a summary for you to review
before anything is sent; nothing reaches you or the regional office without that
review step completing.

## Response Time

Acknowledge escalations within one business day, even if full resolution takes
longer.

## Regional Office Involvement

Escalate to the regional office only when a branch-level resolution isn't possible,
following the same threshold across every branch.
