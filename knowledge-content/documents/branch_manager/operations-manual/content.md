## Opening a Session Day

Confirm tutor staffing, review the day's expected roster, and make sure the branch's
attendance records are ready to be marked as students check in.

## Managing Enrollment

New enrollments, grade-band placement, and branch transfers all route through you.
Keep enrollment records current so reporting stays accurate.

## Staffing

Each session block needs at least one tutor present. If a tutor cannot make their
shift, arrange coverage before the block starts rather than after.

## Monthly Reporting

Branch managers submit a short monthly summary of attendance rates and any
escalations handled, following the Escalation Procedure for anything unresolved.
