## Grading Is Automatic

You never grade a pre-exam, study answer, or post-exam by hand - the platform grades
every response deterministically the moment it's submitted.

## When a Question Looks Wrong

If a student reports that a question seems ambiguous or has no correct option, use
the in-app report flow rather than telling the student the "real" answer yourself.

## Supporting Struggling Students

If a student is stuck on the same skill across multiple weeks, mention it to the
branch manager - persistent struggle on one skill may need attention beyond what one
session's study plan can address.

## Session Pacing

There's no fixed time limit on study; students move on once they've resolved every
target skill for the week, whether that takes ten minutes or the full session.
