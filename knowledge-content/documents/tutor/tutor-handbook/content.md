# Tutor Handbook

DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION
Synthetic content for development and evaluation only.

## Your Role

Tutors staff a branch during session hours, check students in for attendance, and
are available to help when a student is stuck beyond what the platform's hints cover.

## Before Sessions Start

Confirm the day's roster and make sure attendance can be recorded for every student
expected that day, since a missing record blocks that student's pre-exam.

## During Study

Students may ask you directly instead of using the platform's hint/solution/video
options. Encourage them to try the platform's help first, then step in if they're
still stuck.

## Flagged Questions

If the platform flags a student's study attempt for tutor review after repeated
unresolved tries, check in with that student before they move on.
