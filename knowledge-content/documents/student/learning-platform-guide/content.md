# Learning Platform Guide

DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION
Synthetic content for development and evaluation only.

## Signing In

Sign in with the account your parent or tutor set up for you. If you're a student
with more than one sibling on the platform, you'll only ever see your own sessions.

## Starting a Session

Pick the topic your tutor assigns for the week. If attendance hasn't been recorded
yet, the platform will pause and explain what to do next.

## During Study

Each study question lets you answer, then choose a hint, a full solution, or a video
if you get it wrong. You can always try again after reviewing help.

## If Your Session Is Interrupted

If you lose connection or close the tab, reopening the platform picks up exactly
where you left off - nothing you've already answered is lost.
