## Welcome

This handbook covers what to expect from a typical week at IntelliChoice and how to
get the most out of your study sessions.

## Your Weekly Cycle

Every week you take a short pre-exam, work through a study set built just for you,
and finish with a post-exam. The study set changes based on what you got wrong.

## Asking for Help

It is completely normal to ask for a hint or a full solution during study. Growth
comes from trying, checking your understanding, and trying again - not from getting
everything right the first time.

## Videos

Some study items offer a short video explanation instead of a text hint, if one is
available for that skill.
