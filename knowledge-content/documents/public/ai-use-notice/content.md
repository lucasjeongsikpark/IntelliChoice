# AI Use Notice

DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION
Synthetic content for development and evaluation only.

## Where AI Is Used

IntelliChoice uses AI models in two places: generating hints/solutions during study
sessions, and answering questions in the organization Q&A assistant.

## What AI Does Not Do

AI never grades a student's answers, never decides attendance status, and never
sends an email or calendar invite without a human approving it first.

## Human Review

Every question the AI tutor might ask a student to review goes through automated
checks before it is ever shown, and students can flag anything that looks wrong.

## Model Limitations

The AI assistant only answers from approved organization documents. If it cannot
find a grounded answer, it says so and offers to connect you with a staff member
instead of guessing.
