## What We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to run a learning session: attendance
status, question responses, and topic-level mastery estimates. We do not collect
browsing history or device location outside of the optional branch-locator feature.

## Where Student Records Live

Identity information (names, emails, guardian relationships, attendance) is kept in
a separate system from learning records. Learning platform data references students
only by an internal identifier, never by name.

## AI Use and Your Data

Questions you ask the AI tutor, and the content of hints or solutions it generates,
are not stored with your name attached. See the AI Use Notice for more detail on how
AI features work.

## Requesting Your Data

Parents may request a copy of their child's records, or request deletion, by
contacting their branch manager, who will route the request appropriately.
