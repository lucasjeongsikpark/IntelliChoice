## What a Session Looks Like

When you arrive, your tutor checks you in for attendance. You then take a short
pre-exam on the week's topic, work through a personalized study set, and finish with
a post-exam covering the same material.

## Getting Help During Study

If you answer a study question incorrectly, you can ask for a hint, a full worked
solution, or a short video — whichever helps you understand the step you missed.
Using help is expected and does not count against you.

## What Counts as Progress

Progress is measured by how much your post-exam score improves over your pre-exam
score for the same topic, not by how fast you finish.

## Reporting a Problem With a Question

If a question seems wrong, confusing, or has no correct answer among the choices,
tell your tutor or use the in-app report button. Every report is reviewed.
