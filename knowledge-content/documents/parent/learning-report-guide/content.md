## Reading the Score Comparison

Each report shows a pre-exam score and a post-exam score for the same topic. The
difference between them is the learning gain for that week.

## Independent vs. Supported Correct Answers

The report separates answers your child got right on their own from answers that
needed a hint or a full solution first. Both count as progress, but the split shows
how much support was needed.

## Weak Skills Called Out

Skills below the mastery threshold are listed separately so you can see exactly
which concepts the next study plan will prioritize.

## What the Report Does Not Include

The report never includes raw AI conversation transcripts or any identifying details
beyond your child's name and the topics studied.
