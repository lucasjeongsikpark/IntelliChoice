## Your Role

Parents are responsible for getting their child to sessions on time and reviewing
weekly learning-gain reports. You do not need to help with the material itself -
that's what the tutor and adaptive platform are for.

## Multiple Children

If you have more than one child enrolled, you will be asked to select which child a
session applies to each time you sign in, since reports and progress are tracked
per student.

## Viewing Reports

Learning-gain reports become available after your child's post-exam. They show
pre-exam and post-exam scores per topic, and how much of the study support came from
independent work versus hints or solutions.

## Communicating With Tutors

Tutors are available during session hours at your child's branch. For anything that
cannot wait until the next session, contact the branch manager.
