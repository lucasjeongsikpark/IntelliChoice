DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION
Synthetic content for development and evaluation only.

## Respect in Session

Treat tutors and other students the way you'd want to be treated. Sessions are a
shared space, and everyone is there to learn.

## Using the Platform Honestly

Answer questions with your own understanding first before asking for a hint or
solution. The platform is built to help you learn, not to finish fast.

## Reporting Concerns

If something makes you uncomfortable during a session, tell your tutor or your
parent right away. Branch managers are also available for anything a tutor can't
resolve.

## Consequences

Repeated disruptive behavior during sessions is handled by the branch manager, in
conversation with a parent, following the same process at every branch.
