## General Questions

For questions about enrollment, scheduling, or billing, contact your branch manager
directly using the details in the Branch Directory.

## Technical Issues

If the learning platform itself is not working (login errors, a session that will
not load), use the in-app "Report a Problem" option so it reaches the right team.

## Something Urgent

For anything involving a student's safety or wellbeing, contact your branch manager
immediately by phone rather than email or chat.

## Escalating Beyond a Branch

If a branch manager cannot resolve an issue, they can escalate it to the regional
office; parents and students should not need to contact the regional office directly.
