## What Grades Does IntelliChoice Serve?

We currently serve grades 1 through 7, organized into three curriculum bands:
early (1-2), middle (4-5), and upper (6-7).

## How Do I Enroll My Child?

Enrollment happens through a branch manager. Bring proof of grade level and pick a
weekly session slot that fits your schedule.

## Is There a Placement Test?

Yes. New students take a single placement pre-exam so their first study plan starts
at the right difficulty instead of the very beginning of a topic.

## What Does a Session Cost?

Pricing varies by branch and is set locally; ask your branch manager for the current
rate sheet, since this FAQ does not include pricing.
