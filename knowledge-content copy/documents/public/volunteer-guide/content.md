## Volunteering at IntelliChoice

Branches occasionally accept community volunteers to help with study-hall supervision
and materials setup. Volunteers do not tutor students directly or access any student
learning data.

## Requirements

- Must be 18 or older
- Background check cleared through the branch manager
- One orientation session before the first shift

## How to Apply

Contact your local branch manager using the details in the Branch Directory. Include
your availability and which branch you'd like to support.

## What Volunteers Do Not Do

Volunteers never handle attendance records, grading, or any part of a student's
adaptive learning session. Those responsibilities stay with tutors and the platform.
