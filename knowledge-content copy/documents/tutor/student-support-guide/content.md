## Recognizing When a Student Needs More Help

Watch for students who repeatedly decline hints, guess quickly without reading, or
seem disengaged - these are signs the automated support alone isn't enough.

## Talking to Parents

Share concerns about a student's engagement or progress with their parent directly
during pickup, or ask the branch manager to help schedule a conversation.

## Escalating Wellbeing Concerns

Anything involving a student's safety or wellbeing goes to the branch manager
immediately, following the same escalation procedure at every branch.

## Working With Multiple Learning Styles

Some students benefit more from the video option than text hints; there's no wrong
choice among hint, solution, and video - encourage students to try what helps them.
